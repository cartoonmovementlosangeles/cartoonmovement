CARTOON MOVEMENT

a Los Angeles based workshop series

CARTOON MOVEMENT is an infinite, neverending and free workshop for the public, organized by the Campaign for a Socialist Party.

CARTOON MOVEMENT is oriented around methods of comics composition applicable to all styles and all levels of drawing experience.

That means if you can read this, you can do it!

Sessions are hosted bi-weekly on Mondays at 5:00 PM in West Los Angeles, around the Palms area.

Contact for more details: cartoonmovementlosangeles@gmail.com
