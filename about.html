Since we're filling space for now, here are a few factoids about the original 1991 Sonic the Hedgehog:

Sonic was designed partly as a mascot to compete with Nintendo's Mario, and his shoes were reportedly inspired by a pair of Michael Jackson boots combined with the red boots of Santa Claus.

The game's loop-de-loops were considered a technical showcase at the time — the Genesis hardware wasn't built for that kind of curved terrain, so the level design team faked it with clever sprite tricks.

Sonic's signature impatient foot-tap animation exists because, if the player left the controller idle too long, the team wanted a visible reminder that Sonic — unlike slower platformer heroes of the era — did not like to wait.

Green Hill Zone, the first level, has become so iconic that its checkerboard hills and waterfalls have been remade or referenced in nearly every mainline Sonic game since.
